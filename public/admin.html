Race participants

Upload a CSV of runners. Each runner gets free entries numbered from 2026 5001 up. Recognized columns: Name (or First name + Last name), Email, Phone/Mobile, Bib. A file with only names and no header row also works.

Selling at the event & texting numbers

Volunteers sell on the Sell page: look the buyer up by phone or last name, pick a quantity, then cash, check or card. It prints a receipt slip and texts the numbers (2026 0001, 2026 0002, and so on). Every sale goes straight into the drawing.

Ticket numbers

Every number is 8 digits: the prefix plus 4 digits. Each kind of entry has its own block so numbers never overlap. The raffle screen spins the prefix in quickly and slows down for the last 4 digits.

Card payments (Stripe + iPhone card terminal)

Buyer lookup (Givebutter contacts)

The Sell page searches a copy of your Givebutter contacts by phone number or last name. Pull a fresh copy before the event (and again on race morning). People who already bought at the event are found too.

Send sales to Givebutter (after the race)

Each sale becomes a Givebutter transaction (cash, check or card) with the buyer's name, phone and email, and an internal note listing their ticket numbers, the check number or the Stripe payment. By default nothing is sent during the event: press the button below once sales are done.

GIVEBUTTER_API_KEY isn't set in Netlify, so sales are only kept here.

Reset raffle

Clear every draw (for a practice run), clear everything including tickets and participants, or clear the practice sales. Type RESET to confirm.

“Clear all sales” deletes every ticket sold on the Sell page (use it after practicing) so race-day numbers start again at the first number. Real card sales must be voided (refunded) first.
